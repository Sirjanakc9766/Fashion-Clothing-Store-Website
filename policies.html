Our Policies

Everything you need to know about shopping with us.

Shipping Policy

Orders are processed within 1–2 business days and delivered across Nepal. Delivery times vary by location, and a tracking update is shared once your order is on its way.

Returns & Exchanges

  • Items can be returned or exchanged within 7 days of delivery
  • Products must be unused, unwashed, and in their original packaging with tags attached
  • Sale items are eligible for exchange only, not refund
  • Refunds are issued to the original payment method after inspection

Payment Policy

We accept cash on delivery as well as major online payment methods at checkout. All prices listed are in Nepalese Rupees (Rs.) and are inclusive of applicable taxes.

Privacy Policy

Your personal information is used only to process orders and improve your shopping experience. We do not sell or share your data with third parties beyond what's required to fulfill your order.
